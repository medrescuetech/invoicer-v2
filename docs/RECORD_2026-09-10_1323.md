## Commits Applied

### 1. `6cf4356` — Add quote support, explicit N/A due dates, and release v2.0.8

**Scope:** Domain, persistence, service, documents, UI, settings, tests, packaging.

- Added `InvoiceStatus.QUOTED` and quote-aware status derivation.
- Added `QTE` prefix and `next_quote_number` sequence to `NumberingService`.
- Added `Invoice.is_quote` property (derived from number prefix).
- Added `InvoiceService.issue_quote()` and `InvoiceService.convert_quote_to_invoice()`.
- Introduced `_UNSET` sentinel in `InvoiceService.create_draft()` / `create_custom_draft()` so an omitted due date uses payment terms while an explicit `None` means N/A.
- Updated PDF, Word and Excel document generators to use quote-specific wording and display `N/A` when `due_date is None`.
- Added quote wording and next-quote-number settings to `SettingsDialog`.
- Integrated quote/N-A workflow into `InvoiceEditorDialog`:
  - Document-type selector (Invoice / Quote).
  - N/A due-date checkbox.
  - Quote defaulting to N/A due date.
  - Upgrade-to-invoice action.
- Updated `InvoiceListPage` and `MainWindow`:
  - New Quote buttons/menu items.
  - Quote status filter.
  - Quote-to-invoice upgrade action.
  - Guards on invoice-only actions (payments, receipts, credit notes, reminders) for quotes.
- Bumped version to `2.0.8` in:
  - `pyproject.toml`
  - `src/invoice_manager/app.py`
  - `InvoiceReceiptManager.spec`
  - `installer/InvoiceReceiptManager.iss`
- Added/updated tests:
  - `tests/domain/test_numbering.py` — `QTE` sequence.
  - `tests/domain/test_statuses.py` — quote status handling.
  - `tests/integration/test_invoice_service.py` — quote lifecycle and conversion, N/A due date.
  - `tests/pdf/test_invoice_pdf.py` — quote PDF output and N/A due date.
